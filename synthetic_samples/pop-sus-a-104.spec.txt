# A suspension replaces the third rather than adding a note to the triad,
# which is a different ranking problem from every colour #287 and #274 weigh
# (#600). This package states it the way a guitar band plays it: the sus4 for
# a bar, its resolution the next, on the tonic and on both its neighbours.
# pop-sus-triads-a-104 is the same spec with the plain triad in every
# suspended slot, and its drums and bass are note-for-note this package's.
# Its comping is not: the arranger voices each chord nearest the one before
# it, so from the first sus2 onward the two grids voice their shared bars at
# different pitches (#611). The two rows are therefore not comparable on
# quality; each package's own row stands.
# ChordQuality holds both suspensions; whether either can be decoded and
# ranked is the open question.
