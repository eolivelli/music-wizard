Measured while fixing #196, and worth separating from #187.

#187 is about the *spacing* of the chart's bar lines. This is about their *phase*. `ChartLayout` takes the anchor from the beat grid's **first** downbeat, and on a recording with a lead-in that beat is placed where the tracker has the least evidence — before the groove has started.

Measured on `samples/blues-a-90bpm.mp3` (local-only), per-bar root accuracy of the **emitted chart** (`tools/score-chart.py`) with `--first-downbeat` moved across the four candidate phases, on two beat grids that differ in only 27 of their 453 beats:

```
downbeat phase   before #196   after #196
beat 0              87.6%        71.7%
beat 1              70.2%        91.2%
beat 2              93.0%        96.5%
beat 3              96.5%        97.3%
```

*(Corrected: the `beat 1` before-cell first read 93.0%, which was `beat 2`'s value copied up a row. Found in review of #234. Both columns are reproducible with `tools/score-chart.py` and `--first-downbeat`.)*

**The harness reading swings by 27 points on which of four beats the anchor lands on**, and the shipped answer — whichever beat the tracker emitted first — is the worst of the four on both grids. The recording's first four beats are wrong on both: its true grid starts at 0.081 s and the two trackers put their first beat at 0.000 s and 0.325 s respectively, then converge by 2.75 s.

So this is one beat deciding a whole chart, and neither the model score (88.5% to 87.6% over the same change, using every downbeat) nor the beat F-measure against the music (0.970 to 0.977) moves anything like as much.

Two candidate directions, neither measured:

- **Anchor on a downbeat the grid is confident about** rather than the first one, and extrapolate the earlier bars backwards. The rest of the chart is then not hostage to the lead-in.
- **Fix the lead-in in `BeatTracker`** so the first beats are not placed on weak evidence at all. librosa's `__trim_beats` — trim leading and trailing beats whose smoothed onset strength is below half the RMS — was tried on all five benchmarks and **does not help**: it drops nothing on four of them and four beats on the fifth, because the lead-in beats do sit on onsets. They are just at the wrong phase.

The first is where the leverage is, and it belongs with #187 and #66.
