hanno-ucciso-luomo-ragno-karaoke.mp3

"Hanno ucciso l'uomo ragno" (883), karaoke version, 4:31. Pop, simplified
arrangement, no published chords to hand. Fetch locally:
  yt-dlp -x --audio-format mp3 --audio-quality 0 \
    -o uncommitted/hanno-ucciso-luomo-ragno-karaoke.mp3 \
    https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Lqq8ye8bItE
First real commercial pop recording tried end to end (2026-08-01). MW read
159 BPM in 4/4 and a chart dominated by the Am-F-C-G loop; Enrico, who knows
the song, judged it a very good job by ear. Known weaknesses on this track:
Am/A7/A flip-flopping on sustained A chords, and the final ~10 bars degrade
into chromatic noise (fade-out, or the beat drift of #196).
