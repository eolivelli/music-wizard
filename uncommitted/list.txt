    https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XXOIqni93AY
Remembered by ear (explicitly uncertain): essentially A E D E throughout,

Run 2026-08-01. MW read 89.1 BPM in 4/4 and a chart whose whole vocabulary is
G#, D#, C# with occasional D#7 and A#7 — exactly the remembered A, E, D (plus
an occasional B7-shaped dominant) transposed down one semitone, so the harmony

song, inter-beat gaps from 0.28 s to 1.4 s, the #196 pattern), so the printed
89 BPM is a median over an unsteady grid. The final ~10 s (fade-out) degrades
into low-confidence chromatic noise: A#m F7 C7 G.

remembered as an up-tempo rock song, so a beat-tracking octave error (half
the true tempo) is plausible but unconfirmed — worth checking by ear against
the render.
Enrico's verdict (2026-08-01): the sequence "looks pretty good", confirming
the progression is right, but he remembers the song as being in Em, not G —
classic relative-minor confusion. MW's G-major reading (G-D-Em-C, tonic G) and

set of chords, just disagree on which chord is heard as "home"; the chart's
harmony is not contradicted, only its major/minor framing.
